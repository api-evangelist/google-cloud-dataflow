The UK is transitioning from AI experimentation to production-scale agentic systems, with Google Cloud announcing partnerships with HSBC (AI-powered wealth management and financial crime detection), startup Ineffable Intelligence, and Deloitte on an AI Studio in London. Google Cloud emphasizes its full integrated stack of custom silicon, frontier models, and infrastructure supporting an agentic enterprise transition projected to generate 400 billion pounds in economic value for the UK by 2030.
